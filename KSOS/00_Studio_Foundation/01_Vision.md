# KSOS 01 - Vision

> Status: Living document (to be expanded iteratively)

## Purpose
Define the enduring direction and identity of the studio.

## Vision Statement
Build the world's most trusted research-driven preschool studio that creates joyful experiences children love and parents confidently recommend.

## Why This Exists
AI can generate content rapidly. KSOS exists to ensure every creative decision is guided by child development, parent trust, and long-term thinking rather than short-term metrics.

## Guiding Principles
- Child-first decisions
- Parent trust compounds
- Learning through play
- Discovery before explanation
- Philosophy before technology

## Scope of this Document
This chapter will evolve into the foundational reference for every strategic decision made by the studio, including vision hierarchy, success definition, decision filters, and long-term direction.
